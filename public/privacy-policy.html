Privacy Policy for CMS Engineer Portal

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

Last Updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which CMS Engineer Portal ("the Application", "we", "our") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from registered field engineers and service technicians ("Users"). This application is an internal enterprise tool deployed for servicing banking machines, cash counters, and associated financial hardware.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Application, we collect the following categories of information to perform authorized field service operations:

  • Account & Profile Information: User ID, full name, official email address, phone/WhatsApp number, assigned regional base city, and engineer operational role.
  • Precise & Background Location Data: Periodic GPS coordinates, route transit points, speed, and timestamps recorded during active duty shifts.
  • Camera & Photo/Video Evidence: Photos of defected machines, diagnostic logs, serial number plates (TID), resolution completion documents, and optional fault videos.
  • Expense & Financial Voucher Files: Images of travel receipts, toll-tax slips, fuel receipts, and courier tracking notes.
  • Device & Network Information: Device model, operating system version, network connectivity status, battery percentage, and unique application session identifiers.
Important Notice on Location Tracking:
The Application utilizes background location tracking (via an explicit Android Foreground Service) solely while an engineer is on duty transit to assigned bank branches. This information is strictly used to verify site attendance, calculate travel mileage allowances, and optimize dispatch routes. Location tracking automatically ceases when you log out or end your duty shift.

2. How We Use Collected Information

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate enterprise purposes, including:

  • Dispatching and scheduling complaint tickets to the nearest available technician.
  • Verifying physical technician arrival at bank branch locations for audit and security.
  • Processing spare part requests, warehouse dispatch tracking, and float inventory deductions.
  • Validating engineer travel mileage claims against actual transit GPS logs.
  • Maintaining audit logs of machine repairs, component serial replacements, and resolution slips.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent Users' personal identification information to third parties. Information is strictly shared within our secure enterprise ecosystem:

  • Management & Dispatch Operations: Authorized internal branch managers, dispatch supervisors, and workshop in-charges have access to repair tickets and transit reports.
  • Bank Audit Teams: Anonymized ticket resolution documentation may be submitted to partner financial institutions to verify complaint resolution SLA compliance.
  • Legal & Compliance: When strictly required by law or judicial process.

4. Data Storage and Security

All communications between the mobile application and our central database servers are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Sensitive data is stored on secured servers with restricted database credentials, role-based access control, and automated daily backups.

5. User Data Retention & Account Deletion

Service records and ticket logs are retained for auditing and regulatory compliance purposes as required by banking maintenance contracts. If an engineer departs the organization, their account is deactivated. Users may request review or deletion of their personal contact details by contacting their system administrator at support@qmstraders.com.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is strictly an enterprise business solution intended for certified adult technicians aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. When updated, the revised date at the top of this page will be updated. Users are encouraged to frequently check this page for any changes.
